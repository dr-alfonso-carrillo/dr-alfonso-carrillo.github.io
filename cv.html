Curriculum Vitae

Dr. Alfonso J. Carrillo holds a M.Sc. in Chemical Engineering from Universidad de Salamanca, a M.Sc. in Renewable Energies by Universidad de León, and a Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from Universidad Rey Juan Carlos. His Ph.D. research was conducted at IMDEA Energy (Spain) focusing on oxides for solar thermochemical energy storage. Then, he moved to the Electrochemical Materials Laboratory to perform his postdoctoral research on redox materials for solar-to-fuel thermochemical conversion, first at ETH Zurich (Switzerland), and after at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, MIT (USA), where he was 2018 Eni-MIT Energy Fellow. He has received the Energy and Environmental Research Grant by Fundación Iberdrola (2014), Juan de la Cierva Formación fellowship (2018) by the Spanish Ministry of Science, and in 2020, the Junior Leader Fellowship by Fundación laCaixa. He was awarded three times as one of the best presentations in the Annual Symposium of Young Researchers at IMDEA Energy (2012, 2013, and 2015). He has recently been selected as Emerging Investigator 2024 by the journal Chemical Communications from the Royal Society of Chemistry and received the prestigious Leonardo BBVA grant 2024. In 2025 he obtained the Ramón y Cajal grant, however he renounced to it since in the meantime he obtained a position as CSIC Tenured Scientist, which he officially helds since January 2nd 2026. He is co-author of more than 45 publications, which have reached more 2500 citations (h = 24, ten as corresponding author, eighteen as first author, 75% Q1 SJR), with high impact in the fields of solar thermochemical energy storage and exsolution in energy materials. He has co-authored two book chapters, and he has acted as main editor of a special issue at Frontiers in Energy Research. He has participated in several national and international conferences, giving seminars at institutions such as Imperial College London, UK, IREC and ICMM, Spain, HZB and TUM, Germany or University of Messina, Italy. In addition, in 2019 he organized the symposium Photoassisted Processes/Solar Fuels at the International Conference of Electroceramics, held in Lausanne, Switzerland and in 2025 the Exsolution for Sustainable Energy Materials inside MATSUSFall25 in Valencia. Furthermore, in July 2022, he gave invited talks at the Energy & Fuels symposium in the Solid State Ionics International conference (July 2022) held in Boston, USA, and in September 2022 a keynote talk at the 3rd SPHERA III summer school on Solar Thermochemistry at ETH Zurich. Since January 2019, he works at the Energy Conversion and Storage group at ITQ, where he is has supervised one PhD thesis (2025) and currently supervising another one. Additionally, he has supervised two Master Thesis at ETH Zurich and two at ITQ, and several undergraduate students at IMDEA Energy, MIT and ITQ. He participates as a mentor in the CAMINO mentoring program at CSIC. He is member of the Dissemination Committee at ITQ, and he has served as member of the 2021, 2022 and 2023 ITQ’s Winter Meeting Scientific Committee, and he is the ITQ's representative for open science in SOMMA. He has acted as proposal evaluator for M-ERA.net 2024, Polish Academy of Science or Spark’s Atmospheric Methane Research Exploratory funding (USA). Since his PhD stage, he has been very active in funding acquisition, and has raised 421000 € from competitive research calls. In addition, he has participated in several projects funded by European (FP7, Horizon Europe, M-ERA.net), Spanish (Fundación Areces, MINECO) or American calls (MIT Energy Initiative, MISTI Global Seed Funds), as well as research projects funded by private entities (Exxon, USA). Furthermore, he has been awarded three times beamtime access as main proposer at ALBA synchrotron and once at BESSY II in Germany.
